## Brand & Style

This design system draws visual provenance from the pages of ancient palm-leaf manuscripts (*tāḷapatra*), hand-bound astrological journals (*pañcāṅga*), and high-end literary printing traditions of the Indian subcontinent. It reinterprets Vedic astrology not as esoteric mysticism or algorithmic fortune-telling, but as a deliberate, classical discipline rooted in timeless astronomical geometry, philosophy, and human reflection.

The visual direction embraces **Warm Editorial Minimalism**. It rejects the ubiquitous neon nebulas, synthetic violet gradients, glassmorphic glows, and automated chatbot aesthetics common to contemporary horoscope apps. Instead, the interface relies on tactile parchment undertones, hairline editorial rules, generous whitespace, and classical typographic rhythm.

### Personality & Tone
- **Scholarly & Contemplative:** Calm, measured, and respectful of tradition without slipping into historical kitsch.
- **Human & Archival:** Feels printed on physical archival paper; content breathes with expansive margins and disciplined hierarchy.
- **Precise & Mathematical:** Planetary tables, *kuṇḍalī* (birth chart) frameworks, and divisional data are presented with razor-sharp editorial clarity.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural mineral pigments historically used in manuscript illumination: terracotta (*sindoor* / clay), burnt antique gold, deep alizarin maroon, and charcoal iron-gall ink.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FAF7F0` (warm ivory parchment)
  - Sunken / Recessed: `#F4EFE6`
  - Elevated Paper / Cards: `#FFFDF9`
- **Surface Containers & Framing:**
  - Container Low: `#F5EFE1`
  - Container Mid: `#EFE8DA`
  - Container High: `#E8DFCE`
- **Typography & Content:**
  - Primary Text: `#2B241D` (deep bistre charcoal)
  - Body Text: `#3D342C` (warm deep umber)
  - Secondary / Archival Notes: `#736659` (muted warm sepia)
  - Tertiary / Captions: `#8C7D6F` (weathered parchment earth)
- **Accents & Significators:**
  - Primary Accent: `#9E4733` (terracotta red for critical indicators, ascendant markings, and primary calls to reflection)
  - Secondary Accent: `#A88133` (antique gold for planetary dignitaries, auspicious highlights, and astronomical degrees)
  - Tertiary Accent: `#6E2628` (deep muted maroon for editorial banners, retrograde status, and traditional glyphs)
  - Supporting Accent: `#D9822B` (subtle saffron for transition moments and seasonal markers)
- **Rules & Dividers:**
  - Hairline Rule: `#E2D9C8`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-contrast literary serif display titles with clear, neutral humanist sans-serif for reading comfort. **Playfair Display** renders astrological houses, titles, and verse citations with dignified poise. **Noto Sans** provides seamless visual harmony across Latin text, IAST diacritics (e.g., *Śani*, *Rāhu*, *Nakṣatra*), and native Devanagari script (e.g., शनि, नक्षत्र, लग्न).

### Editorial Usage Rules
- **Display Headings:** Always set with intentional leading; avoid tight line compression. Major section openers may use small caps for labels directly above the headline.
- **Editorial Subtext:** Use `editorial-italic` sparingly for classical verse translations, philosophical reflections, or interpretive syntheses.
- **Numerals & Planetary Degrees:** Numerical astrological degrees (e.g., `24° 18' 32"`) must align tabularly inside charts and data listings.
- **Devanagari Integration:** Maintain matched cap-height and optical weight between Devanagari headings and English titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is disciplined, bookish, and asymmetrical where needed to honor the cadence of historic manuscripts. Content conforms to an editorial grid anchored by generous margins.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column grid, max content container of `1200px` centered, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, and 64px (`4rem`) outer margins. Editorial reading columns span 7–8 columns; chart matrices and technical ephemeris tables occupy the full 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 24px gutters and 32px margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 20px outer margin. Astrological charts reflow into single-column expandable accordion panels.

### Rhythm
Vertical spacing prioritizes editorial rhythm over dense enterprise dashboard patterns. Major thematic segments are separated by generous expanses (`space-3xl` or `space-4xl`) punctuated by single hairline rules or centered astrological asterisks (*śrī-bindu* / small four-point diamond glyphs).

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects synthetic drop shadows, elevated floating pills, and diffuse glow effects. Visual hierarchy is achieved exclusively through **tonal paper layering** and **delicate hairline boundaries**.

### Layering Principles
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#FAF7F0` acts as the untinted archival parchment surface for the entire viewport.
- **Nested Card / Inset Plate (Level 1):** `#FFFDF9` sits flat on the canvas, delineated by a single `1px solid #E2D9C8` boundary. No box shadow is applied.
- **Sunken / Analytical Regions (Level -1):** Ephemeris tables, planetary degree monitors, and chart house interiors use `#F4EFE6` or `#EFE8DA` to convey an engraved or debossed manuscript plate.
- **Dialogues & Modal Overlays:** Elevated solely through an opaque ivory backdrop (`#FFFDF9`) framed with a double-rule hairline border (`1px solid #E2D9C8` nested inside `4px` padding and an inner `1px solid #E2D9C8`), accompanied by an ultra-subtle warm ambient shadow (`0 8px 30px rgba(43, 36, 29, 0.06)`).

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular is structured, architectural, and almost sharp, mimicking trimmed vellum sheets and crisp brass astronomical instruments. 

- Corner radii remain strictly between `0px` and `4px` (`rounded-sm`).
- Buttons, input containers, and informational cards never use pill shapes (`rounded-full`).
- Traditional North Indian (*Diamond*) and South Indian (*Square*) chart representations are drafted with clean right angles and 1px geometric divisions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid background of terracotta red (`#9E4733`) with crisp white-ivory text (`#FFFDF9`), `radius: 2px`, padding `10px 24px`. On hover, shifts to deep maroon (`#6E2628`) with zero elevation jump.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a `1px solid #736659` border, primary charcoal text (`#2B241D`), and subtle beige hover state (`#F4EFE6`).
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Unbordered, bistre or antique gold (`#A88133`), underlined by a hairline rule offset by 4px.

### Cards & House Quadrants
- Surfaces use `#FFFDF9` bordered by `#E2D9C8`.
- Astrological chart plates feature an optional subtle inner corner ornament or corner tick marks (`3px` line accents in `#A88133`).
- Cards avoid saturated badges; status tags rely on plain uppercase labels (`label-sm`) with a light tinted background (`#F5EFE1`).

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- Background: `#FAF7F0` or `#FFFDF9`.
- Border: `1px solid #D8CEBA`.
- Focus State: Border color shifts to antique gold (`#A88133`) with a clean `1px` outline offset. No soft blue or purple halos.
- Placeholder text in warm sepia (`#8C7D6F`).
- Labels sit above the field in `label-sm` with letter-spaced small caps.

### Astrological Chart (*Kuṇḍalī*) Display
- Rendered with precise SVG vector paths in `1px` or `1.5px` strokes (`#736659`).
- Ascendant (*Lagna*) denoted with terracotta tag `#9E4733`.
- Benefic and malefic designations indicated through typographic weights and dignified glyphs rather than harsh red/green badges.

### Lists & Ephemeris Tables
- Striped alternating backgrounds are prohibited. Rows are delineated solely by hairline horizontal rules (`#E2D9C8`).
- Numbers, longitudinal degrees, and retrogradation symbols (`℞`) align cleanly to the right using tabular figures.
- Column headers are displayed in `label-sm` muted sepia.

### Chips & Filter Tokens
- Flat rectangular or subtly softened corners (`rounded-sm`).
- Unselected: Surface `#F5EFE1`, text `#3D342C`, border `#E2D9C8`.
- Selected: Surface `#3D342C`, text `#FFFDF9`, border `#3D342C`.
